About

What Is This?

Pony Games is a community-run directory for pony fan games, game jam events, and free development resources. We aim to be the central place where players discover games and developers find tools.

FAQ

Is Pony Games free?

Yes. Pony Games is a free, non-profit fan project. We don't sell the games ourselves: every listing links to where the developers share their game, and almost all pony fan games are free to download.

Do I need an account?

Not to browse, play or download. You need one to like and bookmark games, write reviews and join a game's discussion. Your bookmarks then follow you to all your devices, and you can collect gold stars and badges. Create one here.

A game is missing. How do I add it?

Use the Submit a game form (also in the Games menu). Fill in what you know, add a few screenshots and the download links. A moderator checks every submission before it goes on the site. You don't have to be the developer to submit a game.

Some info about a game is wrong or outdated.

Open the game's page and click the ✎ icon at the top right ("Suggest a correction"). The form is already filled in with the current info, so you only change what's wrong, and you can reorder or add screenshots too. The game shows "Change under review" until a moderator has looked at it.

A download link is broken.

Click the 🏳 icon on the game's page and choose "Broken or dead link". If you know a working link, please add it in the details, or suggest a correction with the new link.

What is the bounty board?

The bounty board lists games we know existed but can't find a working copy of. If you have one of them (or know where it lives), send a tip from its wanted poster. There's no reward except the eternal gratitude of the herd, and maybe a gold star if you have an account.

What are gold stars and badges?

Thank-yous from the moderators for helping the site: submitting games, fixing info, finding lost games. They're given by hand and show up next to your name on reviews and messages, and on your account page.

Why are some games hidden or blurred?

Games tagged NSFW or with gore are hidden from the lists by default and their pictures are blurred until you choose to see them. You can show them with the filters on the games page. If a game is missing such a tag, please report it.

Can I review a game more than once?

You can write one review per game, but you can edit or delete it at any time, on the game's page or on your account page. The same goes for your messages in a game's discussion.
